# Title Suggestions

## Title Criteria Used

Strong biomedical titles should be specific, searchable, accurate about study design, avoid overclaiming, foreground the clinical or methodological contribution, and include key terms readers will search: `pediatric growth`, `electronic health records`, `remeasurement`, `anthropometry`, `longitudinal`, `measurement error`.

## Recommendation

Use **#1** if the target journal is informatics, pediatrics, or EHR-focused:

**Prospective Remeasurement Alerts for Suspicious Pediatric Growth Measurements in Electronic Health Records**

It is clear, searchable, clinically grounded, and does not claim to identify "true errors."
